# Patch 7.35d Notes

## Sven
- Base armor increased by 2.
- Warcry duration increased from 8s to 10s.
- Sven is now incredibly strong against physical damage lineups.

## Meepo
- Poof damage reduced from 100/120/140/160 to 80/100/120/140.
- Meepo is slightly weaker in the early game.

## Drow Ranger
- Multishot cooldown reduced by 2 seconds.
- Drow Ranger's farming speed has slightly improved.

## Chaos Knight
- Chaos Strike lifesteal reduced from 30/40/50/60% to 20/30/40/50%.
- Chaos Knight is now more vulnerable during teamfights if focused.

## Puck
- Illusory Orb speed increased by 50.
- Puck has an easier time dodging spells now.
